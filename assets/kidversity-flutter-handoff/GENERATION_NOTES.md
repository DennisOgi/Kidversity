Final Mandarin and Play artwork was regenerated after the audience clarification. Earlier character illustrations were rejected and are not in the runtime asset folder. Generated art may use more dimensional shading than the original flat-style prompt; the supplied files are the reviewed result. All image files retain their original pixels. No guaranteed trademark exclusivity is asserted for the logo concept.
